How to play

Tap a book you can reach: one at the end of a shelf, beside a gap, or beside a book already home. Neighbours of the same colour come along and go to their own spots; books with nowhere to go yet wait on the book cart. Fill every spot to finish the picture. A Bookmark takes back a pull, a Step Stool reaches any one book, and an Extra Cart adds two cart spaces. The rules are also in the game's Settings.

Purchases

The 72 bookcases of the six rooms are free. The Rare Books Wing and Quiet Hours are bought once; the Book Tote (a pack of tools) can be bought again. Bought something before, or on another device with the same Apple Account? Open Settings → Restore Purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
