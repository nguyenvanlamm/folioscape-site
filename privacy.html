Privacy Policy

Folioscape · last updated 31 October 2026

Folioscape is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the bookcases you have finished, your “On time” stamps, your tools and the rewards already given.
  • Your settings: whether Quiet Hours is on.
  • Whether you have bought the Rare Books Wing, Quiet Hours or a Book Tote, and which purchases were already delivered.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

The 72 bookcases of the six rooms are free. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
